Starship Explorer

Rotate – drag with one finger
Zoom – pinch
Pan – two-finger drag
Info – tap any part or label

Stack / Ship / Booster / Raptor – choose what to view (tap the active one again for a summary card).
Skin – Solid → Cutaway (half-section that always faces you) → X-ray.
Fuel flow – animated LOX (blue) and methane (orange) from tanks to engines.
Explode – pull the stages and sections apart.
Parts – show/hide tanks, plumbing, engines, structure, heat shield, fins & flaps.

Model tag – shows which Starship version is drawn (V3 approximation or the retired Block 2); tap it for the differences and to switch.
